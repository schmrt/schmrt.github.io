Welcome to the College of Magic and Sorcery

Greetings, aspiring sorcerers. I am Dean Big Blue, and it is my honor to welcome you to Utah State University's newest and most extraordinary academic college. Whether you are drawn to the ancient arts of spellcasting, the precise science of potion-making, or the noble discipline of magical creature studies, you have found your home. Our college is committed to preparing the next generation of magical practitioners for careers in enchantment, defense, and beyond.

Why Study Magic at USU?

  • World-class faculty with centuries of combined spellcasting experience
  • State-of-the-art magical laboratories equipped with the latest enchanted instruments
  • A welcoming community of witches, wizards, and everything in between
  • Internship partnerships with leading magical institutions across the realm
  • Located in the heart of the mountains, where the magical energy is strongest

Explore Our Programs

From undergraduate certificates to advanced doctoral degrees, the College of Magic and Sorcery offers a full range of programs to match your ambitions. Whether you are just beginning your magical journey or looking to specialize in a focused discipline, we have a path for you. Visit our Programs page to learn more.
