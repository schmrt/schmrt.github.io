Undergraduate Programs

The College of Magic and Sorcery offers three undergraduate degree paths, each designed to give students a rigorous foundation in both magical theory and applied practice.

  • Bachelor of Arts in Spellcraft and Enchantment
  • Bachelor of Science in Potions and Alchemical Sciences
  • Bachelor of Arts in Magical Creature Studies

All undergraduate students complete the College's core curriculum in their first year, which covers the history of magic, basic incantations, and an introduction to magical materials. Specialization begins in the second year.

Graduate Programs

For students seeking advanced mastery, the College offers two graduate-level degrees.

  • Master of Magical Arts (M.M.A.)
  • Doctor of Sorcery (D.S.)

Graduate students work closely with faculty on original research. Recent thesis topics have included the thermodynamics of fire spells, the ethics of memory charms, and a comparative study of Eastern and Western dragon-taming traditions.
